      FAQ
      Questions before you join
      
    
      
      
      Does the team own any $MING?
      No. Team allocation: 0%.
There are no team tokens, no private allocation and no reserved supply. The team does not receive any of the minted $MING.
$MING starts with the community.
      
      
      Where does the mint money go?
      95% goes directly into the liquidity pool, and the LP is burned.
The other 5% goes to the treasury wallet for development, listings, community initiatives and ecosystem activity.
The contract has no owner and no withdraw function, so nobody can touch the pool funds.
      
      
      Can the team dump on the community?
      There are no team-held tokens to dump.
No team allocation. No private sale. No hidden wallet. No team unlock.
The only $MING the treasury gets is the 2% trade fee. It may sell that to fund the project, and every treasury transaction is public on-chain.
      
      
      What if the mint doesn't sell out?
      Your money isn't trapped.
If $MING does not list within 10 days, every participant can call refund() and get 100% of their USDT back.
If the launch fails, the treasury receives nothing.
      
      
      When do I receive my $MING?
      Immediately. Your $MING is sent to your wallet in the same transaction as your mint. No claim step.
It stays locked until the pool exists, so nobody can create a market before the official launch. The moment share 5,000 is minted, it is yours to trade.
      
      
      What happens after listing?
      $MING is fully open to the market. Anyone can buy or sell on PancakeSwap, with or without an Agent ID.
There is no special team trading window and no privileged exit.
The market decides the price. The community owns the supply.
      
      
      What is the 2% trading fee?
      A 2% fee is charged on buys and sells through the pool. Wallet-to-wallet transfers are free.
The full 2% goes to the treasury wallet and pays for development, the team building $MING, DEX listings, community initiatives and Agent ID holder rewards.
The rate is fixed in the contract and can never be increased.
      
      
      Is $MING an investment?
      No. $MING is a community token with no promised returns.
The price can go up or down, and you can lose everything you spend.
Only participate with what you can afford to lose.
